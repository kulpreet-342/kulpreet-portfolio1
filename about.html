My Story, Hobbies & Education

From a curious student in Ellenabad, Haryana to studying Mechanical Engineering at JECRC University — fueled by rockets, MIT lectures, cartoon illustrations, and manga.

"Every complex machine began as an audacious question: 'How does it work, and how can we push it further?'"

I was born and raised in Ellenabad, Haryana. From my early days, I was captivated by the physical world and how things move, accelerate, and interact. While others were satisfied simply looking at the sky, I wanted to know the exact mechanics of how colossal metal rockets could break through Earth's gravitational pull and soar into space.

My schooling at Nachiketan Public School in Ellenabad laid a strong foundation of discipline, curiosity, and scientific thinking. It was there that I discovered my passion for mathematics and physical sciences, which naturally steered me toward engineering.

Today, I am in my first year pursuing B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at JECRC University, Jaipur. Here, I am delving deeper into the core principles of mechanics, thermodynamics, and design, driven by an unyielding ambition to work with advanced machines, rocketry systems, and innovative engineering frontiers.

✨ What Inspires Me: Hobbies & Interests

Curiosity & Creativity
🚀

Rockets & How They Work

My greatest passion! I love deconstructing the physics of rocket propulsion, de Laval nozzles, regenerative cooling, staged combustion, and orbital trajectories. Understanding how thousands of tonnes of metal defy gravity inspires my engineering vision.

🎓

Watching MIT Lectures

I spend hours watching MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW) lectures on Classical Mechanics, Linear Algebra, and Aeronautics. Learning from world-class professors like Walter Lewin fuels my self-taught technical hunger outside college hours.

📖

Reading Manga

Avid reader of manga! I admire the rich storylines, persistence against overwhelming odds, intricate visual line-art, and futuristic themes that continually motivate my imagination and resilience.

🎨

Drawing Cartoons & Sketches

Skilled at drawing expressive cartoons and character illustrations. Blending art and engineering gives me superior spatial awareness, attention to visual proportions, and a creative outlet to communicate ideas vividly.

🎨 Creative Showcase: The Cartoonist's Touch

Drawing cartoons isn't just a leisure hobby for me — it trains my hand-eye coordination, isometric drafting intuition, and storytelling ability. In engineering, great designers don't just calculate; they visualize!
